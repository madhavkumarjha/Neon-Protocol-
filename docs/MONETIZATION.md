# MONETIZATION.md — Post-V1 Monetization & Economy Strategy

> Ethical monetization frameworks, cosmetic design, rewarded video ad integration, and KPI activation triggers for Neon Protocol.

---

## 1. Monetization Core Philosophy

1. **Retention First, Monetization Second:** No monetization systems will be introduced into V1 until core player retention targets (Day-1 retention ≥ 25%, Day-7 retention ≥ 8%) are validated.
2. **Strictly Non-Pay-To-Win:** Gameplay balance, weapon stats, and leaderboards must remain 100% skill-driven. Money cannot purchase raw power or leaderboard rank.

---

## 2. Monetization Pillars (Post-V1)

### A. Cosmetic Cyberware (Skins & FX)
- **Weapon Skins:** Neon chrome, digital camo, synthwave gradient finishes.
- **Dash Trail FX:** Custom particle trails (Matrix code rain, fiery exhaust, rainbow laser).
- **Player Models:** Alternative cyber-cyborg avatars.

### B. Rewarded Video Ads (Non-Intrusive)
- **One Extra Life per Session:** Watch a 15-second rewarded video to revive once with 50% HP per endless run.
- **Upgrade Reroll:** Watch a rewarded video to refresh the 3 upgrade choices during a wave clear.

---

## 3. Activation Triggers

Monetization features remain disabled until the following KPI benchmarks are hit:
- **DAU:** ≥ 1,000 active daily players.
- **Average Session Length:** ≥ 7 minutes.
- **Leaderboard Engagement:** ≥ 20% score submission rate.
